Why this font (see DESIGN.md for the full history):
  - Exact matched pair for the QUL "Indopak Nastaleeq" text this app
    ships - font and text come from the same lineage, which is what
    keeps mark positioning correct.
  - Verified against the actual bundled text: 0 missing codepoints out
    of the 71 the text uses (844 codepoints in the font).
  - Verified via fontTools GDEF inspection: all five marks that other
    Nastaleeq fonts misclassify (U+0614, U+0615, U+0617, U+06D6, U+06ED)
    are correctly class 3 (MARK) here, plus U+06E1, U+0670, U+0652.
    No mark-collision and no large-circle rendering bug.
  - This font was briefly rejected earlier in development for drawing
    U+06E1 as a closed circle rather than an open hook. That is no
    longer material: after the text was re-sourced to QUL's IndoPak
    Hanafi dataset, U+06E1 appears only 26 times in the whole Quran
    (the text uses U+0652 standard sukun 62,360 times instead).
