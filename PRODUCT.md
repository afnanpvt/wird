# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single private user: a 21-year-old Muslim building a daily Quran-reading habit, using the app on their own Android phone in short sessions throughout the day. No accounts, no other users, no cloud sync. The primary job on any screen is quick, low-friction daily reading with an honest sense of progress. Young and energetic in taste, not a minimalism-at-all-costs user — happy to see tasteful gamification (streak fire, a pettable cat) as long as it has personality and doesn't feel cheap.

## Product Purpose

A personal Quran reading and habit-streak tracker. It exists to help the user read a little every day and keep doing so, with forgiving streak mechanics (a grace day) rather than punishing ones, and with progress numbers that reflect real completion of the Quran rather than inflated activity counts.

## Brand Personality

Bold and energetic, but still Apple-disciplined: dark, high-contrast, typography-led (large confident numerals, strong hierarchy), not cluttered. Think Apple Fitness/Health's dark mode energy rather than Apple Notes' quiet cream. Gamification is welcome and encouraged in a couple of focal moments (streak fire once a streak is worth celebrating, a full-body animated cat with idle behaviors the user can pet) — the discipline is in keeping those moments crafted and purposeful, not in avoiding them.

## Anti-references

Cheap-feeling gamification: generic progress bars, cluttered badge walls, confetti spam, aggressive push-notification guilt. Also avoid cluttered dashboards and cards nested inside cards. The bar is craft, not restraint for its own sake.

## Design Principles

- Typography carries hierarchy and energy: bold weights, confident scale, tight letter-spacing on big numerals.
- A few well-crafted, purposeful moments of delight and gamification (streak fire, cat pet) rather than either systemic gamification or none at all.
- Progress metrics must be honest: distinguish reading activity from actual Quran completion.
- Dark, high-contrast surfaces by default; typography and a single vivid accent do the work, not decoration.
- Every screen should still feel fast for a 30-second daily visit, not a dashboard to manage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum. Respects system text scaling and system light/dark mode. No additional specific accessibility needs identified.
