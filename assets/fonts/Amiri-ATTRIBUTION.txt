Used for the "Uthmani" (AmiriQuran.ttf) and "Tajweed" (AmiriQuranColored.ttf,
color-coded per Tajweed recitation rules) script options.

Known gap: no glyph for U+065E (ARABIC FATHA WITH TWO DOTS), used ~1807
times (0.26% of characters) in the paired Uthmani text - those spots fall
back to the system font. Everything else is covered, with correct GDEF
mark classification confirmed via fontTools before adopting.
