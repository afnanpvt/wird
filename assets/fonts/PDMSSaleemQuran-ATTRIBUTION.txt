This font was chosen after comparing four Indo-Pak Nastaleeq candidates
glyph-by-glyph. It is the only one that gets BOTH things right at once:

  1. Correct GDEF mark classification (no combining-mark collisions -
     the bug that forced an earlier retreat to generic Naskh).
  2. Correct artwork for U+06E1 (the jazm/"jera" mark used ~62,000 times
     throughout the Quran, functioning as the primary Indo-Pak
     no-vowel marker) - drawn as the authentic open hook, not a closed
     circle. Two other candidates (the previously-bundled "AlQuran
     IndoPak by QuranWBW", and a v4.2.2 variant of the same lineage)
     draw this specific glyph as a plain circle, which is not the
     Indo-Pak Mushaf convention.

Known gap: this font has no glyph for U+0614 (ARABIC SIGN TAKHALLUS),
used in exactly 5 places in the whole Quran (6:124, 12:108, 36:30,
41:44, 59:2 - the classical "Sakta" pause markers). Those 5 spots will
render via system font fallback instead of this font. Every other
codepoint the app's Indo-Pak text actually uses is covered - verified
by checking every unique codepoint in assets/data/quran_ar_indopak.json
against this font's cmap.

The font previously bundled here (AlQuran IndoPak by QuranWBW) carried
an equivalent restriction ("NOT FOR... DISTRIBUTION... WITHOUT WRITTEN
NOTICE BY QURANWBW.COM"), so this is not a new category of risk, just
a continuation of an existing one - authentic Indo-Pak Quran fonts are
essentially never released under FOSS-compatible licenses, for reasons
tied to concerns about textual accuracy and tampering with the Quran.

If distributing via F-Droid: tag the app with the NonFreeAssets
anti-feature (https://f-droid.org/en/docs/Anti-Features/). This is the
standard, common path for Quran apps generally.

If a truly FOSS-licensed Indo-Pak Nastaleeq font with correct mark
shapes surfaces later, prefer it over this. Checked and ruled out
during this evaluation: KFGQPC Nastaleeq (correct U+06E1 glyph, but
misclassifies 5 other marks as base glyphs in GDEF, causing collisions
- and its own EULA is also non-free, just more redistribution-
permissive: free copy/distribute, no modification).
